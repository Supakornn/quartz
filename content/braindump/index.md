Sometimes you learn something useful but don't have time to write a proper blog post about it. Or maybe you solve a problem and want to remember how you did it. That's what this section is for - capturing knowledge quickly without worrying about perfect formatting or complete explanations.

These notes are meant to be:

- Easy to find
- Practical and actionable
- Personal reminders
