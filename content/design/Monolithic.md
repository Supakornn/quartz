### Overview

- **Defination**: Monolithic architecture is a single unified application where all application's modules are combined into one codebase and deployed as a single unit.

- **Advantages**:
	- Simple to develop
	- Easy to test
	- Strightforward development

- **Disadvantages**:
	- Hard to scale specific components
	- One bug in one module can crash entire system
	- Harder to maintain as the codebase grows
